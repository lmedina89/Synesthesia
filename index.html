Your first mini-lesson

①
Meet middle C

C4 is called middle C. It's the white key immediately left of the first pair of black keys in this octave.

②
The musical alphabet

The white notes are C, D, E, F, G, A, B, then C again. The pattern repeats.

Why this matters for Balamb Garden: Learn where notes live on the keyboard, then connect those keys to printed notes using Read music. You can start with treble clef, like you used for violin, and tackle bass clef later.
